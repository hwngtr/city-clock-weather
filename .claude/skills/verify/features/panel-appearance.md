# Panel position and appearance

## Sub-features

- Place the indicator in the left, center, or right panel section.
- Set its position within the section.
- Choose clock format, temperature units, opacity, and weather-icon color.
- Separate city groups with a vertical bar.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open **City Clock & Weather settings…** from the panel indicator. Change the controls under **Panel position**, **Display**, and **Time and appearance**.

## Driving it with the GNOME Shell preferences window

Move the indicator to another panel section and position. Change the clock format and Fahrenheit switch. Confirm that the indicator moves and its displayed time and temperature update. Toggle **Follow system accent color** and confirm that the weather icon changes color while text follows the panel theme.

## Gotchas

- The color setting applies to weather icons. It does not set the text color.
- The clock display uses the selected city's IANA time zone, not the computer's time zone.
